Why it matters

Paper can be reused

Paper can be recycled several times, which saves trees and water.

Plastic lasts

Plastic bottles take hundreds of years to break down if they are thrown away.

Glass never wears out

Glass can be melted and made into new jars again and again.

What we can do at school

  • Rinse bottles and cans before recycling them.
  • Use both sides of the paper before it goes in the bin.
  • Bring a reusable water bottle.
  • Put up labelled bins in every classroom.
